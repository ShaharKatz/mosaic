# Open Questions

> One-line scope: unresolved cross-cutting product or architecture questions.
>
> Area affinity: cross-area
> Part of [Architecture docs](README.md)

> Ask the owner when a new material ambiguity is not already settled by a recorded decision; record a durable question here if it outlives the work. A recorded decision remains settled unless new facts invalidate its rationale; an open question alone does not reopen it.

---

## Open questions

The questions recorded here describe genuinely undecided product or architecture choices, conflicts, or externally unverifiable state, not approvals or evidence that a planned feature has shipped. Cite the source intent and a current in-repository observation; name the owner who must settle the question before it is settled into element-doc prose or promoted to an obligation.

Each entry uses a subject heading `### OQ-<n> — <title>`, numbered in document order and cited as "open question OQ-<n>", with these parts:
- **Question or contradiction:** the undecided choice, conflicting claims, or state that cannot yet be verified.
- **Sources:** the source intent and current in-repository evidence.
- **Settle:** the named owner and the ruling or external verification needed.
